# Dragostika Worldbuilding Notes

Source reference: `Dragostika_Worldbuilding_Notes_July_2026.md`

## Core Vision

Dragostika is not just an RPG. It is a living world designed to feel like home.

The goal is not for players to beat the game. The goal is for players to want to live there, return to it, and miss its people when they are away.

## Cosmology

- Everyone awakens in this world without knowing where they originally came from.
- The world's origins are intentionally mysterious.
- Even the gods may themselves be creations within a greater design.
- Dungeon Masters become the Supreme Being for their own version of Dragostika.

## World Design Principles

- Start with one village.
- Expand organically through exploration.
- Players create the map through discovery.
- The world continues moving when the player is absent.

## The Village

The village is the emotional center of the setting. It should feel similar to returning to a favorite novel or television series.

Goals:

- Around 50 memorable villagers.
- Additional adventurers for hire.
- Every NPC has dreams, flaws, routines, fears, ambitions, and history.

## NPC Philosophy

NPCs are not dialogue trees. NPCs are people.

Each NPC should include:

- Biography
- Personality
- Psychological archetype
- Values
- Goals
- Secrets
- Knowledge
- Rumors, true and false
- Boundaries
- Schedule
- Relationships
- Speech style
- Vocabulary
- Things they never reveal
- Things revealed only after trust
- Life events
- Memory of the player

## Hidden Relationship System

No visible persuasion skill.

Every important NPC maintains hidden relationship values that change based upon:

- Conversation
- Honesty
- Keeping promises
- Morality
- Payment
- Loyalty
- Reputation
- Shared adventures

These hidden values influence whether NPCs:

- Join adventures
- Share secrets
- Offer discounts
- Heal
- Refuse requests
- Become lifelong friends
- Leave permanently

## Living World

NPCs continue living when the player is elsewhere.

Possible outcomes:

- Marriage
- Death
- Injury
- New jobs
- Adventures
- Betrayals
- Friendships
- Rumors
- Personal successes or failures

Rumors become discovery tools.

## Replayability

Every playthrough should feel different.

Ideas:

- Random NPC locations
- Time-of-day schedules
- Random village encounters
- Different adventurers available
- Different rumors
- Different life events

Opportunity cost matters.

## Solo And Tabletop

The solo game introduces the setting.

Later, tabletop campaigns allow players to revisit familiar people and places with a human Dungeon Master.

## NPC Laboratory

Before adding an NPC to the game:

- Build the complete profile.
- Stress-test them in conversation.
- Ensure they remain consistent.
- Refine until they feel genuinely alive.

## AI Philosophy

AI should perform NPCs, not invent them.

The authored data remains canonical.

AI simply acts within those boundaries.

## Future Ideas

- Discord lore historian
- Specialized AI agents, such as historian, innkeeper, cartographer, quartermaster
- Text-first interactions
- Premium voice later if desired

## Long-Term North Star

Create a world that players revisit because they miss its people.
