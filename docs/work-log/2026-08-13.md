## Foundry JSON character viewer

### Objective

- Build a polished public character-sheet viewer that accepts a Foundry VTT
  Actor JSON export and automatically presents either an ARS/OSRIC or D&D 5e
  character as a compact, readable, printable sheet.
- Make the experience work well on phones while retaining the visual character
  of a sheet a player could print and bring to the table.

- A new, standalone static public utility under `character-viewer/`.
- Browser-only file reading and parsing. Character JSON must not be uploaded,
  persisted to the backend, or written into the existing character vault.
- Separate visual treatments and data adapters for ARS/OSRIC and Foundry D&D
  5e/SRD 5.2 exports, behind one import experience.
- No backend, database, Drago Table, Foundry world, Foundry module, existing
  character builder, deployment, or live-service changes.

## Character Viewer derived OSRIC combat values

### Objective and scope

- Correct the read-only Foundry Actor importer after comparison against five
  current ARS exports showed that the serialized actor-level AC remains 10 even
  when armor is equipped, bow damage lives on a separate ammunition item, and
  ARS does not serialize calculated Strength or Dexterity combat adjustments.
- Limit changes to the public Character Viewer importer and its focused
  verification coverage. Preserve the existing sheet layout and all unrelated
  Drago Table, backend, mapping, follower, and character-vault work.

### Baseline and repository state

- Branch: `codex/first-edition-mapping`.
- Baseline commit: `289a218ce474b0c9e0083f8fc5ac385e465f61e7`.
- The local branch matches its configured upstream at the last successful

### Evidence and intended correction

- All five exports contain `system.attributes.ac.value: 10`, including armored
  characters. Equipped armor instead records its base AC at
  `item.system.protection.ac`; equipped shields record their AC improvement in
  the same field. The viewer must calculate the worn AC from those item records
  and apply the OSRIC Dexterity defensive adjustment.
- Gilvhar's Long Bow intentionally has an empty damage formula. His separate
  Arrow weapon record contains `1d6` damage against both normal and large
  targets. The viewer must resolve ammunition damage for ranged launchers while
  retaining the launcher's rate and ranges.
- The exports contain raw Strength and Dexterity scores but no calculated
  combat modifiers. Apply the published OSRIC ability tables: Strength affects
  melee hit and damage, while Dexterity affects missile hit and armor class.
  Strength 16 is +0 to hit and +1 damage; Strength 17 and above begins granting
  a positive melee hit adjustment.

### Planned verification

- Add focused importer tests covering equipped armor and shield AC, Dexterity
