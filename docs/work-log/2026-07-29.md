## Product and licensing decisions

- Keep the product name `Drago Table`; do not rename the entire application
  OSRIC.
- Use OSRIC attribution where OSRIC-derived rules are presented, while
  preserving a clean distinction between Drago Table branding and licensed
  rules content.
- Do not treat the labels `1e` or `First Edition` as proof that copied source
  content is safe. A separate provenance audit is still required before
  distributing a standalone player rules package.
- Do not rush automatic two-way offline synchronization before the practice
  session. Cross-device sync requires durable queued writes, record versions,
  ownership checks, and visible conflict handling. Tonight's safe foundation
  is an account invitation, live-server character saving, and same-device
  unfinished-draft recovery.

## Practice-session checklist

1. Start Drago Table and sign in as DM.
2. Create a test campaign or open the intended practice campaign.
3. Create a new player with a username and assign that player to the campaign.
4. Choose `Create Invite`, copy the invitation, and open it in a private browser
   window or a second device.
5. Confirm the player sees the expected username and campaign, creates an
   eight-character-or-longer password, and enters the player portal.
6. Confirm the invitation cannot be reused.
7. Build part of a character, refresh the page, and confirm the unfinished work
   returns on that device.
8. Save the character and confirm it appears for both the player and DM in the
   invited campaign.
9. With the session stopped, confirm the saved table can be viewed but not
   changed.
10. Start the remote session, confirm the launcher shows the remote connection
    as live, and test table movement, marching order, combat start/round/end,
    dice history, handouts, journals, and character updates.
11. Stop the session and confirm the player table locks again.
