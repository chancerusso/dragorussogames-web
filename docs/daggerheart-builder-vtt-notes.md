## Initial License Read

This is a practical working note, not legal advice.

The Darrington Press Community Gaming License identifies the Daggerheart System Reference Document 1.0 as Public Game Content. The SRD itself also states that it is Public Game Content under the DPCGL.

The license appears to support building Daggerheart-compatible tools and original/homebrew content from the SRD, provided attribution, naming, logo, and prohibited-content rules are followed.

Key caution: VTT sharing is more restricted. The DPCGL says Public Game Content and Adaptive Content may be shared on whitelisted VTT platforms only, and sharing on other VTTs is prohibited unless separately approved in writing by Darrington Press. As of the public license page checked on July 17, 2026, the listed whitelisted VTTs are Roll20, Demiplane, Foundry, Alchemy, and Fantasy Grounds.

This means a private/internal Daggerheart aid for our own table is different from publicly offering Daggerheart content through our own VTT. Public VTT support on our VTT should wait for written approval or whitelist status.

## Builder Direction

A Daggerheart character builder is likely the strongest first step.

It should:

- Use SRD-derived rules and data rather than copying full rulebook prose.
- Walk players through character creation step by step.
- Keep the final sheet easy to read.
- Use small hover/help bubbles explaining sheet areas in plain language.
- Include class, subclass, ancestry, community, trait assignment, starting values, equipment, experiences, domain cards, HP, Stress, Hope, Evasion, and thresholds.
- Track advancement and cards over time.
- Include required attribution and compatibility language.
- Avoid using official trade dress, official logos, official artwork, proprietary maps, or non-SRD book text unless a separate license grants it.

## VTT Direction

For now, the safest path is:

- Build system-neutral VTT capabilities that can support multiple games.
- Build Daggerheart-style private table aids only for personal/private play.
- Avoid public Daggerheart content packs or paid VTT access tied to Daggerheart until DRP approval is obtained.
- Keep homebrew adversary tooling based on original content and SRD mechanics.
- If public Daggerheart VTT support is desired, contact Darrington Press for written approval or whitelist status.
