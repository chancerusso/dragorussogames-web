# Equipment

Equipment is the practical edge between a prepared adventurer and a body in a corridor.

This page is a rules reference, not a shopping cart. Choose what your class can use, pay the listed cost from starting wealth, record weight when your table tracks encumbrance, and write the items on the sheet.

Costs and weights follow the OSRIC equipment lists in compact form. A referee may adjust availability by settlement, culture, temple, guild, or campaign scarcity.

## Recommended Starting Kits

These are examples only. Change them to match class restrictions, starting wealth, and the expedition.

### Fighter

Long sword, dagger, chain mail, shield, backpack, rope, torches, oil, iron rations, waterskin, sacks, and a few iron spikes. If wealth is high, consider a bow, arrows, and better armor.

### Cleric

Mace, staff or hammer, chain mail, shield, holy symbol, holy water if affordable, backpack, rope, torches, oil, rations, waterskin, and sacks. Remember that clerics use blunt weapons.

### Magic User

Dagger, staff, spellbook, backpack, ink, parchment, candles or torches, oil, rations, waterskin, chalk, and sacks. Spend carefully; low starting wealth makes every tool matter.

### Thief

Short sword or dagger, leather armor, thieves' tools, rope, grappling hook if affordable, chalk, hooded lantern or torches, oil, rations, waterskin, sacks, and a small mirror.
