# Equipment

Equipment turns starting wealth into what the character can actually do in play.

Use this step to buy weapons, armour, light, food, containers, tools, and travel supplies. The complete OSRIC equipment reference is kept on one player page so costs and weights stay consistent.

## What To Record

Write each purchased item on the character sheet with its cost paid and weight if encumbrance is being tracked.

Keep special attention on:
- armour and shield
- main weapon and backup weapon
- missile ammunition
- light source and oil
- food and water
- rope, sacks, spikes, and containers
- holy symbol, spellbook, or thieves' tools when your class needs them

## OSRIC Baseline

Equipment costs, weights, weapon damage, missile ranges, armour values, container capacity, and starting money are taken from the OSRIC Chapter I equipment tables.
