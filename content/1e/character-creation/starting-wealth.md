# Starting Wealth

Starting wealth is the coin your character has available before entering play. Roll the amount for your class, then buy weapons, armor, tools, clothing, animals, and supplies from the equipment lists.

Gold is scarce enough to matter. A first-level character is not assumed to own a full adventuring outfit unless the player spends coin on it.

## Drago Russo Rule

After determining wealth, purchase equipment.

Unspent coin remains on the character sheet.

No free adventuring gear is assumed. If you want rope, oil, rations, a backpack, a holy symbol, a spell component pouch, or a spare sack, buy it or explain why the referee has allowed it as treasure or property.

## What Coin Means

Most prices are listed in gold pieces, silver pieces, or copper pieces.

Use these coin notes when reading the equipment page:

- 1 gold piece equals 10 silver pieces.
- 1 silver piece equals 10 copper pieces.
- Platinum and electrum may appear in treasure, but starting purchases usually use gold, silver, and copper.

## Write This Down

What does your character already own?
Flavor only. This does not grant free mechanical gear.

Examples:
- inherited sword
- military gear
- old backpack
- travel clothing

Coin Remaining:
Write the coins left after purchases.
