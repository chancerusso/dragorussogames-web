## Introduction

Class is your character's job in the adventuring party.

Race told you what kinds of characters are available. Class tells you what you can do, what equipment you can use, how you improve, and what rules you must remember during play.

Use this page to choose a legal class, record the starting class details, and understand the advancement track you are entering.

Table usability note:
The class tables are intentionally compact for screen and print. A later table responsiveness pass should add mobile-friendly wrappers or collapsible views without changing the rules content.

## How Classes Work

Check class in this order:

1. Confirm your race allows the class.
2. Check the class minimum ability scores.
3. Check alignment requirements.
4. Record hit die, armor, weapons, spellcasting, and special abilities.
5. Record the experience bonus rule if you qualify.
6. Note advancement limits from race, class, or both.

Prime Requisite:
The ability score most important to a class.

Experience Adjustment:
A bonus to earned experience when the listed ability score requirement is high enough.

Hit Die:
The die rolled for hit points at each level, until the class reaches its listed hit-dice limit.

Weapon Proficiency:
A weapon your character is trained to use.

Non-Proficiency Penalty:
The attack penalty for using a weapon you did not choose as a proficiency.

## Assassin

### Overview

Assassins are stealth killers, spies, infiltrators, and guild-trained murderers.

Choose Assassin if you want a dangerous character built around ambush, disguise, poison, and underworld obligations.

### Prime Requisite

None.

### Minimum Scores

| Ability | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | 12 |
| Dexterity | 12 |
| Constitution | 6 |
| Intelligence | 11 |
| Wisdom | 6 |

### Experience Adjustment

No experience bonus.

### Hit Dice

d6, maximum 15 hit dice.

### Alignment

Any evil alignment.

### Armor

Leather or studded leather. Shields are allowed.

### Weapons

Any weapon.

Weapon proficiencies:
3 at 1st level, plus 1 every 4 levels.

Non-proficiency penalty:
-3.

### Spellcasting

None at low levels.

At 12th level, an Assassin may attempt to use arcane scrolls.

### Special Abilities

- Assassination when the Assassin gains surprise.
- Backstab with thief-allowed melee weapons.
- Disguise as another build, gender, class, or race within reasonable limits.
- Poison knowledge, subject to campaign law and referee rulings.
- Thief abilities as a lower-level thief.
- At higher levels, limited access to secret or restricted languages.
- At 12th level, scroll use.

### Restrictions

Assassins are tied to assassin guild politics.

High-level advancement requires taking dangerous positions from existing assassins.

## Cleric

### Overview

Clerics are armored divine spellcasters and spiritual leaders.

Choose Cleric if you want to heal, protect, turn undead, fight in armor, and act through divine authority.

### Prime Requisite

Wisdom.

### Minimum Scores

| Ability | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | 6 |
| Dexterity | 3 |
| Constitution | 6 |
| Intelligence | 6 |
| Wisdom | 9 |
| Charisma | 6 |

### Experience Adjustment

Wisdom 16+ grants the class experience bonus.

### Hit Dice

d8, maximum 9 hit dice.

### Alignment

Any alignment.

### Armor

Any armor and any shield.

### Weapons

Blunt weapons only: club, flail, hammer, mace, oil, staff.

Clerics may throw hammers, clubs, and oil, but do not use other missile weapons.

Weapon proficiencies:
2 at 1st level, plus 1 every 3 levels.

Non-proficiency penalty:
-3.

### Spellcasting

Clerics prepare and cast cleric spells.

Cleric spells are granted through divine service rather than learned from a spellbook.

### Special Abilities

- Turn undead.
- Bonus spells from high Wisdom.
- Stronghold or temple play at high level.

### Restrictions

Clerics must honor the divine source or alignment they serve.

The referee may restrict spells if the Cleric acts against that service.

## Druid

### Overview

Druids are neutral divine spellcasters tied to nature, balance, weather, plants, animals, and sacred places.

Choose Druid if you want a spellcaster with wilderness identity, strict neutrality, and unusual high-level limits.

### Prime Requisite

Wisdom and Charisma.

### Minimum Scores

| Ability | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | 6 |
| Dexterity | 6 |
| Constitution | 6 |
| Intelligence | 6 |
| Wisdom | 12 |
| Charisma | 15 |

### Experience Adjustment

Wisdom 16+ and Charisma 16+ grant the class experience bonus.

### Hit Dice

d8, maximum 14 hit dice.

### Alignment

Neutral only.

### Armor

Leather armor only. Wooden shields only.

### Weapons

Club, dagger, dart, hammer, oil, scimitar, sling, spear, staff.

Weapon proficiencies:
2 at 1st level, plus 1 every 3 levels.

Non-proficiency penalty:
-4.

### Spellcasting

Druids prepare and cast druidic spells.

They use properly gathered natural materials as part of their spell practice.

### Special Abilities

- Druidic cant.
- +2 saving throw bonus against fire and lightning.
- Druid's Knowledge at 3rd level.
- Wilderness Movement at 3rd level.
- Immunity to fey charm at 7th level.
- Shapeshift at 7th level.

### Restrictions

Druids must be neutral.

At 12th level and above, advancement depends on limited high-level places in the druidic order.

## Fighter

### Overview

Fighters are the main weapon and armor class.

Choose Fighter if you want the most direct combat class, broad weapon use, strong hit points, and long-term martial dominance.

### Prime Requisite

Strength.

### Minimum Scores

| Ability | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | 9 |
| Dexterity | 6 |
| Constitution | 7 |
| Intelligence | 3 |
| Wisdom | 6 |
| Charisma | 6 |

### Experience Adjustment

Strength 16+ grants the class experience bonus.

### Hit Dice

d10, maximum 9 hit dice.

### Alignment

Any alignment.

### Armor

Any armor and any shield.

### Weapons

Any weapon.

Weapon proficiencies:
4 at 1st level, plus 1 every 2 levels.

Non-proficiency penalty:
-2.

### Spellcasting

None.

### Special Abilities

- Best weapon and armor access.
- Bonus attacks at higher level.
- Extra attacks against creatures with less than 1 full hit die.
- Optional weapon specialization if the campaign uses it.
- Stronghold and followers at high level.

### Restrictions

No class alignment restriction.

Race may still limit Fighter advancement.

### Play Experience

Fighter is simple to start and strong for a long campaign.

### Who Should Play This

Choose Fighter if you want the fewest special rules and the clearest battlefield job.

### Beginner Notes

Your choices are mostly equipment, position, and courage.

### Example Character Concepts

- Mercenary captain
- Village champion
- Veteran caravan guard

### Write This Down

Fighter

Strength

Hit Die:
d10

Any armor, any weapons, best combat progression.

## Illusionist

### Overview

Illusionists are arcane spellcasters who specialize in phantasmal magic, deception, perception, and magical misdirection.

Choose Illusionist if you want a fragile but clever caster with a narrower spell identity than Magic User.

### Alignment

### Armor

No armor or shield.

### Weapons

Dagger, dart, oil, staff.

Weapon proficiencies:

Non-proficiency penalty:
-5.

### Spellcasting

Illusionists cast phantasmal spells.

### Special Abilities

- Does not require read magic to read phantasmal script.
- Gains one new spell of the highest castable level after training for a new level.
- May hire followers at 7th level.
- Stronghold play begins at high level.

### Restrictions

Illusionists are extremely fragile in direct combat.

Race options are limited.

## Paladin

### Overview

Paladins are Lawful Good holy warriors with strict conduct rules and powerful defensive gifts.

Choose Paladin if you want a rare, demanding protector who combines martial strength with sacred obligations.

### Prime Requisite

Strength and Wisdom.

### Minimum Scores

| Ability | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | 12 |
| Dexterity | 6 |
| Constitution | 9 |
| Intelligence | 9 |
| Wisdom | 13 |
| Charisma | 17 |

### Experience Adjustment

Strength 16+ and Wisdom 16+ grant the class experience bonus.

### Hit Dice

d10, maximum 9 hit dice.

### Alignment

Lawful Good only.

### Armor

Any armor and any shield.

### Weapons

Any weapon.

Weapon proficiencies:
3 at 1st level, plus 1 every 2 levels.

Non-proficiency penalty:
-2.

### Spellcasting

Paladins gain limited cleric spellcasting at 9th level.

They do not gain bonus cleric spells from high Wisdom.

### Special Abilities

- Improved saving throws.
- Cure disease by touch.
- Immune to disease.
- Detect evil within 60 ft while concentrating.
- Protection from evil aura in a 10 ft radius.
- Lay on hands once per day for 2 hp per Paladin level.
- Turn undead beginning at 3rd level as a good cleric two levels lower.
- Summon warhorse at 4th level.
- Bonus attacks beginning at 8th level.
- Limited cleric spellcasting beginning at 9th level.

### Restrictions

Paladins must remain Lawful Good.

They have strict limits on magic items, must tithe money, donate surplus wealth, and avoid association with non-Good companions except in exceptional circumstances.

### Advancement

## Ranger

### Overview

Rangers are good-aligned wilderness fighters who scout, track, and defend civilization from hostile forces.

Choose Ranger if you want a Fighter-like class with tracking, surprise advantages, enemy damage bonuses, and later spellcasting.

### Prime Requisite

Strength, Intelligence, and Wisdom.

### Minimum Scores

| Ability | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | 13 |
| Dexterity | 6 |
| Constitution | 14 |
| Intelligence | 13 |
| Wisdom | 14 |
| Charisma | 6 |

### Experience Adjustment

Strength 16+, Intelligence 16+, and Wisdom 16+ grant the class experience bonus.

### Hit Dice

d8, but Rangers begin with 2d8 at 1st level.

Maximum 11 hit dice at 10th level.

### Alignment

Any good alignment.

### Armor

Any armor and any shield.

### Weapons

Any weapon.

Weapon proficiencies:
3 at 1st level, plus 1 every 2 levels.

Non-proficiency penalty:
-2.

### Spellcasting

Rangers gain limited druid and magic user spells at 8th level.

They do not gain bonus druid spells from high Wisdom.

### Special Abilities

- Surprised only on 1 on 1d6.
- Surprises enemies on 1-3 on 1d6.
- +1 damage per Ranger level against evil humanoid or giantish foes in melee.
- Tracking at 90% outdoors and 65% in urban or dungeon settings, adjusted by conditions.
- Bonus attacks beginning at 8th level.
- Limited spellcasting beginning at 8th level.
- Special followers and scrying device use beginning at 10th level.

### Restrictions

Rangers must remain good.

They cannot hire servants or mercenaries until 8th level, and then only good-aligned ones.

No more than three Rangers may operate together.

Rangers may not keep more treasure than they can personally carry, including what a mount can carry for them.

## Thief

### Overview

Thieves are scouts, climbers, lock-openers, trap specialists, pickpockets, backstabbers, and readers of strange languages.

Choose Thief if you want the party's main problem-solver for locks, traps, stealth, and dangerous scouting.

### Prime Requisite

Dexterity.

### Minimum Scores

| Ability | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | 6 |
| Dexterity | 9 |
| Constitution | 6 |
| Intelligence | 6 |
| Charisma | 6 |

### Experience Adjustment

Dexterity 16+ grants the class experience bonus.

### Hit Dice

d6.

### Alignment

Any neutral or any evil alignment. Neutral Good is allowed because it includes a neutral component.

### Armor

Leather or studded leather only. No shields.

### Weapons

Club, dagger, dart, oil, sling, and single-handed swords except bastard swords.

Weapon proficiencies:
2 at 1st level, plus 1 every 4 levels.

Non-proficiency penalty:
-3.

### Spellcasting

None at low levels.

At 10th level, a Thief may attempt to use arcane or phantasmal scrolls.

### Special Abilities

- Backstab.
- Climb Walls.
- Find Traps.
- Hear Noise.
- Hide in Shadows.
- Move Quietly.
- Open Locks, Disarm Traps, and Set Traps.
- Pick Pockets.
- Read Languages.
- Thieves' Cant.
- Read Scrolls at 10th level.

### Restrictions

Thief skills rely on proper armor and circumstances.

Backstab requires approaching unseen and striking with an allowed melee weapon.

## Quick Decision Guide

Choose Fighter if you want the simplest combat class.

Choose Cleric if you want armor, support, and divine magic.

Choose Magic User if you want the strongest long-term spell power.

Choose Thief if you want exploration jobs every session.

Choose Paladin or Ranger if you want a demanding heroic class with strict requirements.

Choose Druid, Illusionist, or Assassin if you want a more specialized character with sharper rules.
