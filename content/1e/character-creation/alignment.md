# Alignment

Alignment describes a character's outlook, loyalties, instincts, and usual behavior in the old struggle between order, freedom, good, evil, and self-interest.

It is guidance, not imprisonment. A Lawful character can lose patience, a Chaotic character can keep a promise, and a Neutral character can take a side when the moment demands it.

At the table, actions matter more than labels. Write the alignment on the sheet, then prove what it means through choices, risks, mercy, ambition, fear, and loyalty.

## Alignment Choices

### Lawful Good

Lawful Good characters believe order should protect the innocent and restrain the cruel. They keep oaths, respect rightful authority, and expect leaders to answer for corruption. A Lawful Good knight might defend a village contract, spare a defeated foe, and still refuse a command from a wicked lord.

### Neutral Good

Neutral Good characters value mercy, decency, and practical help over strict systems. They cooperate with law when law serves people and ignore it when it becomes an excuse for harm. A Neutral Good healer might hide refugees, bargain with bandits to save captives, or break a tax seal to feed a starving hamlet.

### Chaotic Good

Chaotic Good characters trust conscience over codes and freedom over obedience. They fight tyrants, bullies, and supernatural evil, but dislike being chained to procedure. A Chaotic Good scout might free prisoners before negotiating, mock a cruel magistrate, or rob a warlord to arm the villagers.

### Lawful Neutral

Lawful Neutral characters believe structure, duty, custom, or command must hold the world together. Good and evil matter less than the office, oath, law, or hierarchy being served. A Lawful Neutral judge might punish a friend, protect a hated rival under truce, or insist the party honor a signed compact.

### True Neutral

True Neutral characters seek balance, survival, or independence from grand moral crusades. Some defend natural equilibrium; others simply avoid being owned by any cause. A True Neutral druid might oppose both a demon cult and a conquering temple, while a mercenary might help the party because the bargain is fair.

### Chaotic Neutral

Chaotic Neutral characters prize personal freedom, impulse, and the right to choose their own path. They are unpredictable, but not required to be foolish or treacherous. A Chaotic Neutral thief might ignore orders, chase a strange rumor, or insult a duke, yet still guard the companions who have earned trust.

### Lawful Evil

Lawful Evil characters use order as a weapon. They honor contracts when useful, build chains of command, and prefer domination that looks legitimate. A Lawful Evil noble might protect the party under guest-right, crush a rival by legal claim, and demand obedience from everyone beneath their station.

### Neutral Evil

Neutral Evil characters place appetite, ambition, and advantage above mercy or honor. They do not need a grand philosophy to do harm; profit, revenge, or survival can be enough. A Neutral Evil assassin might keep a promise for payment, abandon a doomed ally, or spare an enemy only because a future favor is worth more.

### Chaotic Evil

Chaotic Evil characters reject restraint and revel in fear, ruin, or violent appetite. They may follow a leader for plunder or terror, but loyalty rarely survives frustration. A Chaotic Evil raider might burn a shrine, betray a patron, or choose destruction even when negotiation would be easier.

## Drago Russo Guidance

Alignment should support party play.

Do not use alignment as permission for random betrayal, chaotic stupidity, or disruptive evil. A dangerous or evil character can still keep bargains, respect useful allies, and understand that an adventuring party survives by trust.

No alignment is forbidden by this rule. The expectation is that every player chooses an alignment they can play without ruining the shared expedition.

## Write This Down

Alignment:
Write the alignment you chose.

Behavior Notes:
Write one habit that shows the alignment in play.

Party Promise:
Write why this character can adventure with the group.
