# Demi-Shadow Monsters

Demi-Shadow Monsters is an OSRIC Chapter II spell available as: Illusionist 5.

| Components | V,S |
| Casting Time | 5 segments |
| Saving Throw | See below |

### Spell Description

This spell creates partially-real illusory monsters in the same manner as the spell shadow monsters, but the demishadow monsters have 40% of normal hit points rather than 20%; if they are detected as only quasi-real, they inflict 40% of normal damage and are AC 8.
