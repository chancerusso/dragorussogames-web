### Spell Description

Holding out his or her hand, fingers fanned out, the caster evokes a spray of colours identical to those of the prismatic wall. All creatures in the path of the spell will be struck by one of the rays, determined randomly. Colour Effect of Colour 1 Red Deals 20 points of damage (saving throw for half damage). 2 Orange Deals 40 points of damage (saving throw for half). 3 Yellow Deals 80 points of damage (saving throw for half). 4 Green Poison (saving throw or die). 5 Blue Turns to stone (saving throw negates). 6 Indigo Causes insanity (saving throw negates) 7 Violet Creatures sent to another plane (saving throw negates). 8 Two colours Roll twice, ignoring this result
