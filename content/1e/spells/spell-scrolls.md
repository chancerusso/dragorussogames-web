# Spell Scrolls

Spell scrolls hold spells in written magical form. They are valuable because they let a caster carry magic beyond prepared spell slots, but using or copying them consumes the scroll.

## Casting From A Scroll

A spell may be cast from a scroll if the character can use that kind of magic and can read the writing. The spell is released from the scroll and the writing is erased.

The spell entry still matters: range, duration, casting time, saving throw, and effects are those of the spell unless the scroll says otherwise.

## Copying Into Spell Books

Magic-users and illusionists may copy understood scroll spells into spell books when class rules allow it. Copying the spell erases it from the scroll.

Read magic and the character's spell-learning rules matter when interpreting arcane writing.

## Table Use

Scrolls are best treated as single-use spell resources. Record:

- spell namecaster class listspell levelwhether it has been read, cast, copied, or erasedany referee notes about script, curse, or unusual source
