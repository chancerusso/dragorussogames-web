### Spell Description

This spell has the same effect as the magic user spell limited wish, but requires the creation of a phantasmal force prior to casting, which serves as a focus for the spell. Arcane Spells, Level 1 Various Level: Illusionist 7 Range: See below Duration: See below Area of Effect: See below Components: See below Casting Time: See below Saving Throw: See below This spell enables the illusionist to memorise several first level magic user spells in place of one seventh level illusionist spell. The illusionist may substitute a number of magic user spells equal to his or her level minus ten (4 at 14th level, 5 at 15th level, etc.). The mage spells must be chosen at the same time as the Arcane Spells, Level 1 spell is memorised.
