# Levitate

Levitate is an OSRIC Chapter II spell available as: Magic-User 2.

## Spell Entries

### Magic-User 2

**School:** Arcane Transmutation/Alteration

| Duration | 1 turn/level |
| Area of Effect | Caster or one creature |
| Components | V,S,M |
| Casting Time | 2 segments |
| Saving Throw | Negates |

### Spell Description

This spell allows the magic user to levitate himor herself or another person, to a maximum weight of 100 lbs/level. The magic user can move vertically up or down at a rate of 20 ft per round, and can levitate another at a rate of 10 ft per round. The spell may be cast upon an unwilling enemy, but in this case the intended target is entitled to a saving throw. The spell does not allow any sort of horizontal movement (aerial agility level I-see Chapter III), but it is possible for a levitated person to pull himor herself along if there is something upon which to gain purchase, such as a wall.
