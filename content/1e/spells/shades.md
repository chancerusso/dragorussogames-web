# Shades

Shades is an OSRIC Chapter II spell available as: Illusionist 6.

## Spell Entries

### Illusionist 6

**School:** Phantasmal Illusion

| Field | OSRIC Value |

| Area of Effect | 20 x 20 ft |
| Components | V,S |
| Casting Time | 6 segments |
| Saving Throw | See below |

### Spell Description

This spell creates partially-real illusory monsters in the same manner as the spell shadow monsters, but shades have 60% of normal hit points rather than 20%. If they

are detected as only quasi-real, they inflict 60% of normal damage and are AC 6.
