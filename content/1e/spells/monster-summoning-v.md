### Spell Description

This spell functions in the same manner as monster summoning I, but calls 1d2 creatures as determined on the following table, the monsters appearing in 1d3 rounds: d6 Monster Summoned 1 Cockatrice 2 Doppelgänger 3 Hydra (7 heads) 4 Lycanthrope (wereboar) 5 Minotaur 6 Snake, giant poisonous
