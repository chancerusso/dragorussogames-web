# Stone Tell

Stone Tell is an OSRIC Chapter II spell available as: Cleric 6.

## Spell Entries

### Cleric 6

**School:** Clerical Divination

| Field | OSRIC Value |
| --- | --- |
| Class/List | Cleric 6 |
| Range | Touch |
| Duration | 1 turn |
| Area of Effect | 1 cubic yard of stone |
| Components | V,S,M |
| Casting Time | 1 turn |
| Saving Throw | None |

### Spell Description

This spell causes stone to hear and speak. Rock and stones in the area of effect will answer any of the cleric's questions about what has transpired in their immediate vicinity, or what lies behind them.
