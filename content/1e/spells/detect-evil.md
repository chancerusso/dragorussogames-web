# Detect Evil

Detect Evil is an OSRIC Chapter II spell available as: Cleric 1, Magic-User 2.

## Spell Entries

### Cleric 1 (Reversible)

**School:** Clerical Divination

| Field | OSRIC Value |
| --- | --- |
| Class/List | Cleric 1 |
| Range | Caster |
| Duration | 1 turn + 5 rounds/level |
| Area of Effect | Path 10 ft wide, 120 ft long |
| Components | V,S,M |
| Casting Time | 1 round |
| Saving Throw | None |

### Spell Description

This spell allows the cleric to discern emanations of evil (or good, in the case of the spell's reverse) within the spell's

area of effect, a beam-like pathway. The spell detects evil, not danger, so it will be useless to discover such things as a mindless trap or a poisoned wineglass. Neither will it detect a cursed item unless the nature of the curse is such that the cursed item becomes inherently evil.
