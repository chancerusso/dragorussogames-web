### Spell Description

This spell functions in the same manner as monster summoning I, but calls 1d2 creatures as determined on the following table, the monsters appearing in 1d3 rounds: d10 Monster Summoned 1 Devil, Erinyes 2 Hydra, 8 headed 3 Manticore 4 Ogre Mage 5 Rakshasa 6 Troll 7 Wight 8 Wraith 9 Wyvern 10 Lycanthrope, weretiger
