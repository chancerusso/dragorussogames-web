# Emotion

Emotion is an OSRIC Chapter II spell available as: Illusionist 4.

### Spell Description

By casting this spell, the illusionist is able to instil others with one of four powerful emotions, each with a different effect, as described below: Fear: If the illusionist chooses to instil fear, the spell's effect is similar to that of the fear spell, but with a -2 penalty applied to saving throws. Hate: If the illusionist instils hate, the targets gain a +2 to saving throws, attacks, and damage. Hopelessness: The illusionist causes despair in the target creatures, who will wander sadly away or surrender in the face of a challenge such as a combat. Rage: Creatures affected by rage attack at +1, gain a damage bonus of +3, and gain a bonus of 5 temporary hit points. Temporary hit points are lost first if the creature incurs damage. The affected creature will not willingly retreat from any combat or any opponent.
