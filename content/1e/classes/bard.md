# Bard

## Overview

Bard is an Advanced / Special Entry class.

It is not chosen the same way as Fighter, Cleric, Magic-User, or Thief at first level. A Bard follows a special qualification path before becoming a Bard.

## Quick Facts

Entry:
Advanced / Special Entry.

Role:
Lore, charm, support, combat experience, and druidic magic.

Complexity:
Very high.

Best For:
Experienced players who want a long-form character path.

## What Bard Is

A Bard is a rare character who begins through earlier adventuring careers before entering the Bard class.

The class combines fighting experience, thief-like background, lore, charm, and druidic spellcasting.

## How Qualification Works

The Bard path is not selected as a normal starting class.

The character must qualify through earlier class experience before entering Bard.

Use this page to understand the path, then confirm the exact campaign permission with the referee before beginning.

## Ability Requirements

Bard has steep ability requirements.

The important abilities include Strength, Intelligence, Wisdom, Dexterity, and Charisma.

Do not begin a Bard path unless your ability scores satisfy the campaign's Bard requirements.

## Alignment

Bards must fit the alignment requirements of the class path and its druidic connection.

Confirm alignment before committing to the path.

## Armor

Bards have more limits than ordinary fighters.

Armor use depends on the Bard stage and campaign interpretation of the class path.

## Weapons

Bards begin with combat experience from earlier training, but final weapon use should be recorded from the Bard rules used at the table.

## Spellcasting

Bards eventually gain druidic spellcasting.

They do not begin as ordinary clerics or magic-users, and they do not use the same spell progression as those classes.

## Special Abilities

- Lore and knowledge.
- Influence through music, poetry, or performance.
- Combat background from earlier training.
- Thief background from the qualification path.
- Druidic spellcasting after entering Bard.
- Strong social and support role when played carefully.

## Restrictions

- Advanced entry path.
- Strict ability requirements.
- Requires referee approval before play.
- Not selected as a normal first-level class.
- Requires careful recordkeeping across the qualification path.

## Advancement

Bard advancement begins after the character qualifies for Bard entry.

Record the earlier class path, the point of entry into Bard, Bard level, Bard abilities, spell access, and any special restrictions.

## Play Experience

Bard is a long-term campaign character.

The payoff is flexibility and identity, but the cost is complexity and delayed entry.

## Who Should Play This

Choose Bard if you want an advanced First Edition path and enjoy managing a character over many sessions.

Avoid Bard if you want a simple first character or a class chosen entirely at character creation.

## Beginner Notes

Bard should not be treated like a normal starting option.

For a first character, choose a standard class first unless the referee is explicitly running a Bard-friendly campaign.
