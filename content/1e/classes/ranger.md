# Ranger

## Overview

Rangers are good-aligned wilderness fighters who scout, track, and defend civilization from hostile forces.

Choose Ranger if you want a Fighter-like class with tracking, surprise advantages, enemy damage bonuses, and later spellcasting.

## Prime Requisite

Strength, Intelligence, and Wisdom.

## Minimum Scores

| Ability | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | 13 |
| Dexterity | 6 |
| Constitution | 14 |
| Intelligence | 13 |
| Wisdom | 14 |
| Charisma | 6 |

## Experience Adjustment

Strength 16+, Intelligence 16+, and Wisdom 16+ grant the class experience bonus.

## Hit Dice

d8, but Rangers begin with 2d8 at 1st level.

Maximum 11 hit dice at 10th level.

## Alignment

Any good alignment.

## Armor

Any armor and any shield.

## Weapons

Any weapon.

Weapon proficiencies:
3 at 1st level, plus 1 every 2 levels.

Non-proficiency penalty:
-2.

## Spellcasting

Rangers gain limited druid and magic user spells at 8th level.

They do not gain bonus druid spells from high Wisdom.

## Special Abilities

- Surprised only on 1 on 1d6.
- Surprises enemies on 1-3 on 1d6.
- +1 damage per Ranger level against evil humanoid or giantish foes in melee.
- Tracking at 90% outdoors and 65% in urban or dungeon settings, adjusted by conditions.
- Bonus attacks beginning at 8th level.
- Limited spellcasting beginning at 8th level.
- Special followers and scrying device use beginning at 10th level.

## Restrictions

Rangers must remain good.

They cannot hire servants or mercenaries until 8th level, and then only good-aligned ones.

No more than three Rangers may operate together.

Rangers may not keep more treasure than they can personally carry, including what a mount can carry for them.

## Play Experience

Ranger is a scout and fighter with rules that matter before combat begins.

## Who Should Play This

Choose Ranger if you want wilderness responsibility, tracking, surprise control, and a good-aligned code.

## Beginner Notes

Ranger is not just "better Fighter." It has stricter requirements and real conduct limits.

## Example Character Concepts

- Border warden with old tracks in memory
- Monster-hunter protecting scattered farms
- Exiled noble hiding in the wild
