# Magic User

## Overview

Magic Users are arcane spellcasters with the broadest and most dangerous spell potential.

Choose Magic User if you want fragile early play and enormous long-term magical power.

## Prime Requisite

Intelligence.

## Minimum Scores

| Ability | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | 3 |
| Dexterity | 6 |
| Intelligence | 9 |
| Wisdom | 6 |
| Constitution | 6 |
| Charisma | 6 |

## Experience Adjustment

Intelligence 16+ grants the class experience bonus.

## Hit Dice

d4, maximum 11 hit dice.

## Alignment

Any alignment.

## Armor

No armor or shield.

## Weapons

Dagger, dart, oil, staff.

Weapon proficiencies:
1 at 1st level, plus 1 every 5 levels.

Non-proficiency penalty:
-5.

## Spellcasting

Magic Users cast arcane spells from spellbooks.

A beginning Magic User knows four spells: read magic, one chosen 1st-level spell, and two randomly determined 1st-level spells.

## Special Abilities

- Arcane spellcasting.
- Arcane scroll use.
- Spell acquisition through adventuring and level training.
- Magical crafting at higher levels.
- Stronghold play at 11th level.

## Restrictions

No armor, narrow weapons, low hit points, and dependence on spellbooks.

## Play Experience

Magic User begins weak and becomes campaign-shaping.

## Who Should Play This

Choose Magic User if you like planning, restraint, risk, and long-term power.

## Beginner Notes

At 1st level, your spell choice matters a lot. Your dagger is not your main plan.

## Example Character Concepts

- Nervous apprentice with a stolen spellbook
- Tower scholar seeking forbidden names
- Wandering astrologer with one real spell
