# Paladin

## Overview

Paladins are Lawful Good holy warriors with strict conduct rules and powerful defensive gifts.

Choose Paladin if you want a rare, demanding protector who combines martial strength with sacred obligations.

## Prime Requisite

Strength and Wisdom.

## Minimum Scores

| Ability | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | 12 |
| Dexterity | 6 |
| Constitution | 9 |
| Intelligence | 9 |
| Wisdom | 13 |
| Charisma | 17 |

## Experience Adjustment

Strength 16+ and Wisdom 16+ grant the class experience bonus.

## Hit Dice

d10, maximum 9 hit dice.

## Alignment

Lawful Good only.

## Armor

Any armor and any shield.

## Weapons

Any weapon.

Weapon proficiencies:
3 at 1st level, plus 1 every 2 levels.

Non-proficiency penalty:
-2.

## Spellcasting

Paladins gain limited cleric spellcasting at 9th level.

They do not gain bonus cleric spells from high Wisdom.

## Special Abilities

- Improved saving throws.
- Cure disease by touch.
- Immune to disease.
- Detect evil within 60 ft while concentrating.
- Protection from evil aura in a 10 ft radius.
- Lay on hands once per day for 2 hp per Paladin level.
- Turn undead beginning at 3rd level as a good cleric two levels lower.
- Summon warhorse at 4th level.
- Bonus attacks beginning at 8th level.
- Limited cleric spellcasting beginning at 9th level.

## Restrictions

Paladins must remain Lawful Good.

They have strict limits on magic items, must tithe money, donate surplus wealth, and avoid association with non-Good companions except in exceptional circumstances.

## Play Experience

Paladin is powerful but not casual.

The code, charity, alignment, and social restrictions are part of the class.

## Who Should Play This

Choose Paladin if you want a heroic protector with strict limits and visible moral pressure.

## Beginner Notes

Do not choose Paladin just for powers. Choose it because you want the obligations too.

## Example Character Concepts

- Knight sworn to a saint
- Wandering judge in bright mail
- Heir to a sacred warhorse prophecy
