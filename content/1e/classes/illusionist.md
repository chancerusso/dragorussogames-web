# Illusionist

## Overview

Illusionists are arcane spellcasters who specialize in phantasmal magic, deception, perception, and magical misdirection.

Choose Illusionist if you want a fragile but clever caster with a narrower spell identity than Magic User.

## Prime Requisite

None.

## Minimum Scores

| Ability | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | 6 |
| Dexterity | 16 |
| Intelligence | 15 |
| Wisdom | 6 |
| Charisma | 6 |

## Experience Adjustment

No experience bonus.

## Hit Dice

d4, maximum 10 hit dice.

## Alignment

Any alignment.

## Armor

No armor or shield.

## Weapons

Dagger, dart, oil, staff.

Weapon proficiencies:
1 at 1st level, plus 1 every 5 levels.

Non-proficiency penalty:
-5.

## Spellcasting

Illusionists cast phantasmal spells.

A beginning Illusionist starts with four 1st-level spells: two chosen by the player and two determined randomly.

## Special Abilities

- Phantasmal spellcasting.
- Does not require read magic to read phantasmal script.
- Gains one new spell of the highest castable level after training for a new level.
- May hire followers at 7th level.
- Stronghold play begins at high level.

## Restrictions

Illusionists are extremely fragile in direct combat.

Race options are limited.

## Play Experience

Illusionist rewards players who like solving problems sideways.

## Who Should Play This

Choose Illusionist if you enjoy trickery, careful spell use, and fragile-but-clever characters.

## Beginner Notes

Illusions need table conversation. Ask what creatures perceive, what they know, and what the illusion is meant to change.

## Example Character Concepts

- Stage magician with real power
- Gnome tunnel-phantom
- Scholar of impossible light
