# Monk

## Overview

Monks are disciplined ascetics trained for mobility, unarmed combat, mental focus, and unusual survival.

Choose Monk if you want a strict, demanding class with many special rules and very little dependence on ordinary armor or treasure.

## Quick Facts

Role:
Mobile specialist, scout, and unarmed combatant.

Complexity:
High.

Entry:
Special class with strict ability and conduct requirements.

Best For:
Players who want a challenging old-school class with unusual advancement and equipment limits.

## Prime Requisite

Strength, Wisdom, and Dexterity matter heavily for Monk entry and play.

## Minimum Scores

| Ability | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | 15 |
| Wisdom | 15 |
| Dexterity | 15 |
| Constitution | 11 |

## Experience Adjustment

Monks do not use a simple prime-requisite bonus in the same way as the basic class choices.

Record the class requirements first. Apply any table-specific experience adjustments only if the campaign rule says to do so.

## Hit Dice

Monks use small hit dice compared with fighters.

They rely on mobility, saving throws, special defenses, and careful play rather than heavy armor.

## Alignment

Lawful only.

## Armor

Monks do not wear armor.

Armor interferes with the class identity and class abilities.

## Weapons

Monks use a restricted weapon list.

Typical Monk weapons include simple or traditional weapons such as club, dagger, hand axe, javelin, spear, staff-like weapons, and similar referee-approved Monk weapons.

## Spellcasting

None.

## Special Abilities

- Unarmed combat improves with level.
- Movement improves as the Monk advances.
- Armor class improves without worn armor.
- The class gains defensive and mental discipline abilities over time.
- Surprise, stealth, falling, disease, poison, charm, and similar effects may be affected by Monk class features as the Monk advances.

## Restrictions

- Strict alignment requirement.
- Strict ability requirements.
- No armor.
- Limited weapons.
- Limited personal wealth and magic item use compared with most classes.
- Advancement includes special title and rank pressure at higher levels.

## Advancement

Monk advancement is specialized and should be read directly before choosing the class.

The player records level, experience needed for next level, unarmed damage, movement, armor class, special abilities, and any high-level rank restrictions.

## Play Experience

A Monk is not a simple front-line fighter.

You survive by choosing when to engage, using mobility, and remembering your class defenses.

## Who Should Play This

Choose Monk if you want a demanding character with strict limits and a very distinctive table role.

Avoid Monk if you want heavy armor, broad magic item use, or a simple first character.

## Beginner Notes

Monk is legal First Edition material, but it is not the easiest first class.

Ask the referee before choosing Monk in a new campaign, because high-level advancement and conduct limits can matter a lot.
