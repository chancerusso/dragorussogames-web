# Half-Elf

## Overview

Half-Elves are flexible bridge characters.

They keep some elven gifts while gaining a flexible set of class and multi-class choices.

## Ability Considerations

Half-Elves have forgiving ability requirements compared with stricter demi-human races.

Half-Elf scores must fit these limits:

- Dexterity: at least 6
- Constitution: at least 6
- Intelligence: at least 4
- Strength, Wisdom, and Charisma: normal 3-18 range

## Allowed Class Considerations

Allowed single classes:

- Assassin
- Cleric
- Fighter
- Magic User
- Ranger
- Thief

Multi-class availability:

- Cleric/Fighter
- Cleric/Ranger
- Cleric/Magic User
- Fighter/Magic User
- Fighter/Thief
- Magic User/Thief
- Cleric/Fighter/Magic User
- Fighter/Magic User/Thief

Unavailable classes:

- Druid
- Illusionist
- Paladin

Special notes:
Half-Elves are one of the best races for players who want a hybrid character.

## Languages

Starting Languages:

- Common
- Elven
- Gnoll
- Gnome
- Goblin
- Halfling
- Hobgoblin
- Orcish

Additional Languages:
Use the additional language allowance from Intelligence when the Languages step tells you to choose them.

What Player Records:
Write `Common, elven, gnoll, gnome, goblin, halfling, hobgoblin, orcish`.

## Movement

Movement:
12"

Write:
120 ft

Meaning:
Used later during exploration and combat. You move at the standard adventuring pace for a man-sized character.

## Vision

Half-Elves have infravision 60'.

They also have limited secret and concealed door detection.

Infravision lets you see heat sources in darkness. It does not replace normal light.

## Special Abilities

Half-Elves have partial resistance to sleep and charm effects.

They inherit some elven awareness without taking on every elven limit.

Write these as race notes:

- Partial resistance to sleep and charm.
- Can notice concealed doors when passing nearby.
- Can find secret or concealed doors when actively searching.

## Restrictions

Half-Elves still have advancement limits.

Thieving abilities still require armor allowed to thieves, even when multi-classed.

## Advancement Notes

Race affects advancement:
Yes.

Level limits:

- Assassin: up to level 11
- Cleric: up to level 5
- Fighter: up to level 8 with Strength 18, level 7 with Strength 17, or level 6 with Strength 16 or lower
- Magic User: up to level 8 with Intelligence 18 or higher, level 7 with Intelligence 17, or level 6 with Intelligence 16 or lower
- Ranger: up to level 8 with Strength 18, level 7 with Strength 17, or level 6 with Strength 16 or lower
- Thief: unlimited

Half-Elves cannot be Druids, Illusionists, or Paladins.

## Play Experience

Half-Elf is flexible but not effortless.

You get flexible build paths, but you still need to check how race affects your chosen class.

## Who Should Play This

Choose Half-Elf if you want:

- A hybrid class concept
- Flexible class access
- Some elven traits
- A character who can adapt to party needs

## Example Character Concepts

- Cleric/Fighter serving two traditions
- Fighter/Magic User trained between worlds
- Ranger with elven senses and human grit
- Magic User/Thief who solves problems quietly

## Beginner Notes

Do not choose Half-Elf because you cannot decide.

Choose it because flexibility is the character concept.
