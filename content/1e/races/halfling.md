# Halfling

## Overview

Halflings are small, quiet, resilient adventurers.

They are excellent scouts and thieves, and they reward careful play more than brute force.

## Ability Considerations

Halflings lean toward Dexterity and away from Strength.

They lose Strength and gain Dexterity.

Halfling scores must fit these limits after racial adjustment:

- Strength: at least 6 and no higher than 17
- Dexterity: at least 8 and no higher than 19
- Constitution: at least 10
- Intelligence: at least 6
- Wisdom: no higher than 17
- Charisma: normal 3-18 range

## Allowed Class Considerations

Allowed single classes:

- Druid
- Fighter
- Thief

Multi-class availability:

- Fighter/Thief

Unavailable classes:

- Assassin
- Cleric
- Illusionist
- Magic User
- Paladin
- Ranger

Special notes:
Halflings are strongest when the player uses stealth, ranged attacks, and careful positioning.

## Languages

Starting Languages:

- Common
- Dwarfish
- Gnome
- Goblin
- Halfling
- Orcish

Additional Languages:
A halfling may learn one additional language for each point of Intelligence above 16, but may never learn more than two additional languages beyond the starting list.

What Player Records:
Write `Common, dwarfish, gnome, goblin, halfling, orcish`.

## Movement

Movement:
9"

Write:
90 ft

Meaning:
Used later during exploration and combat. You move slower than races with 12" movement.

## Vision

Halflings have infravision 60'.

Infravision lets you see heat sources in darkness. It does not replace normal light.

## Special Abilities

Halflings resist magic and poison well.

They are strong with bows and slings.

They can surprise enemies more easily when scouting in the right conditions, especially when lightly armored and away from the main group.

Write these as race notes:

- Bonus on saves against magic and poison, based on Constitution.
- Strong attack bonus with bow or sling.
- Better chance to surprise enemies when lightly armored and scouting alone, far ahead, or with only elves and/or halflings.

## Restrictions

Halflings have narrow class choices.

Their thieving abilities require armor allowed to thieves.

Their size and Strength limits make them less suited to heavy front-line play.

## Advancement Notes

Race affects advancement:
Yes.

Level limits:

- Druid: up to level 6
- Fighter: up to level 4
- Thief: unlimited

Halflings cannot be Assassins, Clerics, Illusionists, Magic Users, Paladins, or Rangers.

## Play Experience

Halfling play is about caution, quiet movement, and choosing the right moment.

You are not built to win every direct fight. You are built to avoid bad fights and make good ones easier.

## Who Should Play This

Choose Halfling if you want:

- A stealthy scout
- A small ranged combatant
- A resilient thief
- A careful dungeon survivor

## Example Character Concepts

- Sling-armed scout from a quiet village
- Thief who survives by patience and luck
- Fighter/Thief who opens the way for the party
- Druid with a strange bond to hidden places

## Beginner Notes

Do not play a Halfling like a small Human Fighter.

Use stealth, scouting, missile fire, and retreat routes.
