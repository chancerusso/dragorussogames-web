# Elf

## Overview

Elves are versatile adventurers with magic, scouting, weapon skill, and supernatural resilience.

They offer strong tools, but they also carry advancement limits and more rules to track.

## Ability Considerations

Elves lean toward Dexterity and away from Constitution.

They gain Dexterity and lose Constitution.

Elf scores must fit these limits after racial adjustment:

- Dexterity: at least 7 and no higher than 19
- Constitution: at least 8 and no higher than 17
- Intelligence: at least 8
- Strength, Wisdom, and Charisma: normal 3-18 range

Check this carefully if you want a durable front-line Elf.

## Allowed Class Considerations

Allowed single classes:

- Assassin
- Cleric
- Fighter
- Magic User
- Thief

Multi-class availability:

- Fighter/Magic User
- Fighter/Thief
- Magic User/Thief
- Fighter/Magic User/Thief

Unavailable classes:

- Druid
- Illusionist
- Paladin
- Ranger

Special notes:
Elves are especially notable for Fighter/Magic User and other fighter, magic, and thief combinations.

## Languages

Starting Languages:

- Common
- Elven
- Gnoll
- Gnomish
- Goblin
- Halfling
- Hobgoblin
- Orcish

Additional Languages:
An elf may learn additional languages only with Intelligence 16 or higher. Intelligence 16 allows one additional language, 17 allows two, and 18 allows three.

What Player Records:
Write `Common, elven, gnoll, gnomish, goblin, halfling, hobgoblin, orcish`.

## Movement

Movement:
12"

Write:
120 ft

Meaning:
Used later during exploration and combat. You move at the standard adventuring pace for a man-sized character.

## Vision

Elves have infravision 60'.

They also have a strong chance to notice or find secret and concealed doors.

Infravision lets you see heat sources in darkness. It does not replace normal light.

## Special Abilities

Elves resist sleep and charm effects.

They gain combat benefits with bows and with common elven swords.

They can surprise enemies more easily when scouting in the right conditions, especially when lightly armored and ahead of the party.

Write these as race notes:

- Strong resistance to sleep and charm.
- Bonus with pulled bows.
- Bonus with longsword and short sword.
- Can notice secret doors when passing nearby.
- Can find secret or concealed doors when actively searching.
- Better chance to surprise when scouting alone, far ahead, or with only elves and/or halflings.

## Restrictions

Elves have class advancement limits.

Thieving abilities still require armor allowed to thieves, even when the Elf is multi-classed.

Their lower Constitution ceiling can matter for survival-focused characters.

## Advancement Notes

Race affects advancement:
Yes.

Level limits:

- Assassin: up to level 10
- Cleric: up to level 7
- Fighter: up to level 7 with Strength 18, level 6 with Strength 17, or level 5 with Strength 16 or lower
- Magic User: up to level 11 with Intelligence 18 or higher, level 10 with Intelligence 17, or level 9 with Intelligence 16 or lower
- Thief: unlimited

Elves cannot be Druids, Illusionists, Paladins, or Rangers.

## Play Experience

Elf rewards players who like options.

You can fight, scout, cast, search, and solve problems, but you need to remember more special rules.

## Who Should Play This

Choose Elf if you want:

- Magic and weapons in one concept
- A capable scout
- Secret door awareness
- A character with flexible tactical options

## Example Character Concepts

- Fighter/Magic User with an old blade and a spellbook
- Silent archer scouting ahead of the party
- Magic User/Thief hunting hidden doors and old wards
- Exile from a strange woodland court

## Beginner Notes

Do not assume Elf is simply Human plus bonuses.

Elf is powerful, but the class and advancement limits are part of the choice.
