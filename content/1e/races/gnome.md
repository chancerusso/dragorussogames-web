# Gnome

## Overview

Gnomes are small underground specialists with strong resistance, useful dungeon senses, and access to illusion-focused play.

They are clever, technical, and a little more complex than Dwarfs.

## Ability Considerations

Gnomes require decent Constitution and Intelligence.

Gnome scores must fit these limits:

- Strength: at least 6
- Constitution: at least 8
- Intelligence: at least 7
- Dexterity, Wisdom, and Charisma: normal 3-18 range

## Allowed Class Considerations

Allowed single classes:

- Assassin
- Cleric
- Fighter
- Illusionist
- Thief

Multi-class availability:

- Fighter/Illusionist
- Fighter/Thief
- Illusionist/Thief

Unavailable classes:

- Druid
- Magic User
- Paladin
- Ranger

Special notes:
Gnomes are the natural choice if you want a small dungeon-wise Illusionist.

## Languages

Starting Languages:

- Common
- Dwarfish
- Gnomish
- Goblin
- Halfling
- Kobold

Additional Languages:
A gnome may learn no more than two additional languages beyond the starting list, even with high Intelligence.

What Player Records:
Write `Common, dwarfish, gnomish, goblin, halfling, kobold`. Also note communication with normal burrowing animals.

## Movement

Movement:
9"

Write:
90 ft

Meaning:
Used later during exploration and combat. You move slower than races with 12" movement.

## Vision

Gnomes have infravision 60'.

Infravision lets you see heat sources in darkness. It does not replace normal light.

## Special Abilities

Gnomes resist magic and poison well, with that resilience tied to Constitution.

They are alert to underground construction.

Write these as race notes:

- Bonus on saves against magic and poison, based on Constitution.
- Better attacks against kobolds and goblins.
- Harder for bugbears, giants, gnolls, ogres, ogre mages, titans, and trolls to hit.
- Can check stonework for slopes, unsafe walls, unsafe ceilings, unsafe floors, depth underground, and north underground.
- Can communicate with normal burrowing animals.

When to use detection:
Tell the referee you are deliberately checking nearby stonework or underground direction.

## Restrictions

Gnome multi-class characters are restricted to leather armor.

Class choices and advancement are narrower than Human.

## Advancement Notes

Race affects advancement:
Yes.

Level limits:

- Assassin: up to level 8
- Cleric: up to level 7
- Fighter: up to level 6 with Strength 18, or level 5 with Strength 17 or lower
- Illusionist: up to level 7 with very high Dexterity and Intelligence, level 6 if both are strong, or level 5 if either is lower
- Thief: unlimited

Gnomes cannot be Druids, Magic Users, Paladins, or Rangers.

## Play Experience

Gnome plays like a problem-solver.

You bring dungeon information, resistance, and unusual class options rather than raw simplicity.

## Who Should Play This

Choose Gnome if you want:

- A clever dungeon specialist
- Illusion magic
- Underground exploration tools
- A small character with strong defenses

## Example Character Concepts

- Illusionist who maps by echoes and shadows
- Fighter/Thief with a miner's eye for danger
- Cleric from a hidden burrow-shrine
- Assassin who uses tunnels and misdirection

## Beginner Notes

Gnome is not just a smaller Dwarf.

Choose it for illusion, scouting, and underground utility.
