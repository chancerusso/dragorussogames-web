# Half-Orc

## Overview

Half-Orcs are physically hard adventurers suited to violence, survival, and dangerous work.

They have strong combat potential, but their social and class limits are real.

## Ability Considerations

Half-Orcs lean toward Strength and Constitution, but suffer heavily in Charisma.

They gain Strength and Constitution, and lose Charisma.

Half-Orc scores must fit these limits after racial adjustment:

- Strength: at least 6
- Constitution: at least 13 and no higher than 19
- Dexterity: no higher than 17
- Intelligence: no higher than 17
- Wisdom: no higher than 14
- Charisma: no higher than 12

## Allowed Class Considerations

Allowed single classes:

- Assassin
- Cleric
- Fighter
- Thief

Multi-class availability:

- Cleric/Fighter
- Cleric/Thief
- Cleric/Assassin
- Fighter/Thief
- Fighter/Assassin

Unavailable classes:

- Druid
- Illusionist
- Magic User
- Paladin
- Ranger

Special notes:
Half-Orcs cannot follow the more magical or idealized class paths.

## Languages

Starting Languages:

- Common
- Orcish

Additional Languages:
A half-orc may learn no more than two additional languages beyond the starting list, even with high Intelligence.

What Player Records:
Write `Common, orcish`.

## Movement

Movement:
12"

Write:
120 ft

Meaning:
Used later during exploration and combat. You move at the standard adventuring pace for a man-sized character.

## Vision

Half-Orcs have infravision 60'.

Infravision lets you see heat sources in darkness. It does not replace normal light.

## Special Abilities

Half-Orcs gain physical toughness and strength from race.

They pair well with class paths that reward violence, danger, stealth, or hard survival.

Write these as race notes:

- Strength and Constitution increase.
- Charisma decreases.
- Infravision 60'.
- Strong fit for Fighter, Assassin, Cleric, and Thief paths.

## Restrictions

Half-Orcs have significant class limits.

Their Charisma penalty can affect social situations, hirelings, and reactions.

For multi-class characters, armor follows the stricter class requirement, while weapons follow the more permissive requirement.

## Advancement Notes

Race affects advancement:
Yes.

Level limits:

- Assassin: up to level 15
- Cleric: up to level 4
- Fighter: up to level 10
- Thief: up to level 7 with Dexterity 17, or level 6 with Dexterity 16 or lower

Half-Orcs cannot be Druids, Illusionists, Magic Users, Paladins, or Rangers.

## Play Experience

Half-Orc play is direct, dangerous, and often socially tense.

You are good when the party needs force, intimidation, or ugly work done under pressure.

## Who Should Play This

Choose Half-Orc if you want:

- A hard combat character
- A Fighter, Assassin, Cleric, or Thief path
- A rough-edged dungeon survivor
- A character with real social friction

## Example Character Concepts

- Mercenary Fighter who survives by strength
- Assassin with a brutal reputation
- Cleric/Fighter from a harsh borderland cult
- Fighter/Thief who handles doors, threats, and dirty work

## Beginner Notes

Do not choose Half-Orc only for physical bonuses.

Be ready for limited class options and lower social ease.

## Quick Decision Guide

Choose Human if you want the simplest path and strongest long-term freedom.

Choose Dwarf if you want toughness, dungeon senses, and a strong underground identity.

Choose Elf if you want weapons, magic, scouting, and secret-door awareness in one package.

Choose Gnome if you want underground expertise, illusion options, and clever utility.

Choose Half-Elf if you want flexible class choices and some elven abilities.

Choose Halfling if you want stealth, scouting, missile fire, and careful survival.

Choose Half-Orc if you want physical power, danger, and a harsher class path.

If you are stuck, choose the race that best supports the class you want next.
