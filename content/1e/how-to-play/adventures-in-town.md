# Adventures In Town

Town play is usually more abstract than dungeon or wilderness exploration. Shopping, hiring, resting, training, gathering information, selling treasure, and seeking spellcasting can often happen between expeditions.

## Abstract Town Activity

OSRIC says most town activities can be handled offstage: note money spent or gained, mark the number of days passed, and resume play when the party is ready to adventure.

Common town activities include:

- buying equipment
- selling treasure
- hiring guides, men-at-arms, or specialists
- recovering hit points
- training for levels
- finding NPC spellcasters
- identifying magic
- gathering rumours

## Played-Out Town Scenes

Some town activities are worth playing directly: interviewing hirelings, bargaining with a temple, visiting a sage, navigating politics, investigating a crime, or entering a dangerous district.

If the party enters a dangerous district such as a thieves' quarter, use dungeon-like expectations: time in turns, cautious movement, possible ambushes, and encounter checks.

## Party Separation

Town scenes often split the party. One character buys equipment, another seeks rumours, and another visits a temple.

The referee should keep scenes moving so no player is left waiting too long.

## Notes

Verified against OSRIC core exploration rules for adventures in town.
