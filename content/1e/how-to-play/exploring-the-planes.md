# Exploring The Planes

OSRIC treats planar exploration as high-level play and intentionally leaves much of the planes open for campaign creation.

This page gives players the baseline expectation: the planes are not ordinary travel.

## Player Expectations

Planar adventures are for experienced characters, usually around 10th level or higher. By then, ordinary campaign-world challenges may no longer be enough.

The normal campaign world is on the Prime Material Plane. Other planes may have different rules for matter, distance, time, magic, survival, and danger.

## What OSRIC Leaves Open

OSRIC does not fully define planar geography in the core rules. The referee decides the structure, inhabitants, hazards, and special laws of the planes in the campaign.

Players should expect that light, food, air, gravity, alignment, magic, and travel may behave differently.

## Table Use

Before entering a plane, write down:

- how the party gets there
- how the party can return
- what protections are active
- what spell durations matter
- what supplies can function there
- what the party knows about local hazards

## Notes

Verified against OSRIC core exploration rules for planar travel. No DRG1e planar house rule is added here.
