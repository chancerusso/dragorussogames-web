# Aerial Agility

Aerial Agility helps the referee and players handle flying creatures, flying mounts, and magical flight.

## Flight Basics

Flying creatures climb at half movement rate and dive at a 45 degree angle, descending 1 ft for every 1 ft forward. Aerial Agility VI creatures ignore these two restrictions.

Diving attacks over 30 ft deal double damage against non-diving targets, including ground targets.

Riding a flying monster usually reduces its aerial agility by one step, except for very large creatures such as dragons.

## Aerial Agility Levels

| Level | Movement Control |
| --- | --- |
| I | Barely flying or gliding; moves like a balloon or glider |
| II | 5 rounds to full speed; turns 30 degrees per round |
| III | 2 rounds to full speed; turns 60 degrees per round |
| IV | Full speed in 1 round; turns 90 degrees per round |
| V | Full speed or stop in 6 segments; turns 120 degrees per round; can hover |
| VI | Full speed or stop in 1 segment; can hover and reverse course with 180 degree turns |

Levels II, III, and IV must move at least half speed to stay airborne.

## Table Use

Use aerial agility when flight position matters: pursuit, turning, diving, hovering, spell targeting, or mounted combat.

For quick rulings, ask whether the flier can stop, turn, climb, or dive fast enough to do what the player wants this round.

## Notes

Verified against OSRIC core exploration rules for aerial agility.
