# Magic

## Purpose

Magic is powerful because it is prepared, timed, and vulnerable.

This section teaches spell memorization, preparation, casting in combat, interruption, scrolls, and recovery.

## What OSRIC Says

Magic-users and illusionists prepare spells from spellbooks. Clerics and druids prepare spells through prayer and meditation. In either case, a prepared spell is held in memory until cast.

| Magic Rule | OSRIC Procedure |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum rest | 4 hours in a quiet place without interruption |
| Memorization time | 15 minutes per spell level |
| After casting | The spell leaves memory |
| Reuse | The spell must be memorized again |
| Duplicate preparation | The same spell may be prepared more than once if enough slots exist |

A 3rd-level spell takes 45 minutes to memorize. Two 2nd-level spells take 60 minutes total. Four 1st-level spells take 60 minutes total. The time is counted after the required rest period.

Spell scrolls may be cast directly, which erases the scroll. Magic-users and illusionists may copy understood scroll spells into spellbooks, and that process also erases the scroll.

In combat, spells must be declared before initiative. The caster begins casting in the caster's initiative segment. The spell completes after its casting time. If the caster takes damage while casting, the spell is lost.

## How It Works At The Table

Magic asks the party to protect time.

Before the dungeon, spellcasters decide what risks they expect. During the dungeon, they guard their remaining spells. In combat, the party must keep pressure off a caster long enough for the spell to finish.

Resting underground to recover spells is possible only if the party can find or make a quiet, defensible place and survive the time cost.

## Example

A cleric needs to prepare one 2nd-level spell and three 1st-level spells after resting. The cleric first needs four uninterrupted hours of rest, then 30 minutes for the 2nd-level spell and 45 minutes for the three 1st-level spells.

The full recovery takes five hours and fifteen minutes if nothing interrupts it.

## Write This Down

Prepared Spells:
Write each spell actually memorized or prepared.

Spell Slots:
Write duplicate spells separately.

Spellbook:
Magic-users and illusionists should record copied spells and scrolls used.
