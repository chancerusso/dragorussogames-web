# Magic

OSRIC magic is prepared before danger and timed carefully during danger. Each spell has its own range, duration, area of effect, components, casting time, and saving throw.

In combat, the declaration and casting-time rules are the most important general procedures.

## Preparing Spells

Spellcasting classes memorize spells according to their class rules. Magic-users rely on spell books; clerics and druids prepare spells through their own class procedures.

The spell description controls its details. Some spells take segments, some rounds, some turns, and some use special casting times.

## Declaring Spells In Combat

Before initiative is rolled, spellcasters must state what spell they intend to cast. As the round proceeds, the caster may choose not to cast, but may not substitute another action.

OSRIC explains this as the caster committing mental preparation that cannot be redirected quickly.

## Casting Time And Interruption

The spellcaster begins casting in the caster's initiative segment. That segment counts as the first segment of casting time. The spell takes effect only after the full casting time is complete.

If the caster suffers damage while casting, the spell is lost. While casting, the caster receives no Dexterity bonus to Armor Class.

## Components, Silence, And Conditions

Spell descriptions list components. If a spell requires verbal, somatic, or material components, the caster must be able to provide them.

Silence, restraint, lost components, armor restrictions, inability to gesture, or inability to speak can prevent casting when the spell requires those things.

## Saving Throws And Magic Resistance

Many spells allow saving throws. The spell description states whether a save is allowed and what it does.

Some creatures have magic resistance or immunity. Apply those monster or spell rules as written.

## Example At The Table

The magic-user declares magic missile before initiative. The caster begins on the party's initiative segment. Because magic missile has a short casting time, it may resolve quickly, but if the caster is damaged before completion, the spell is lost.

## Quick Reference

| Magic Question | OSRIC Baseline |
| --- | --- |
| Is the spell declared? | Must be declared before initiative |
| Can the caster switch actions? | May cancel, but cannot substitute a different action |
| When does casting begin? | Caster's initiative segment |
| When does it take effect? | After full casting time |
| What spoils casting? | Damage while casting |
| Does the caster keep Dex AC bonus? | No, not while casting |
