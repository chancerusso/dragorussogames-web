# Equipment & Encumbrance

Encumbrance is the cost of carrying useful things. OSRIC tracks carried weight because armour, treasure, weapons, tools, and coins change movement and surprise.

Use this page with **[Equipment](/1e/equipment/)**.

## Coins And Weight

Ten coins weigh 1 lb. Treasure is valuable, but coin weight can slow the party before they reach safety.

## Maximum Carry

The most a character can carry is 150 lb plus any additional weight allowed by Strength. A character carrying more than that adjusted limit cannot move.

Bulky items can encumber even when they are not heavy. The referee applies common sense for awkward loads.

## Encumbrance Table

This table assumes a base movement of 120 ft. Small races with base movement 90 ft deduct 30 ft from movement rates, to a minimum of 30 ft, but do not change surprise effects.

| Weight Carried | Max Movement | Surprise Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Up to 35 lb | 120 ft/round | +1 surprise bonus if armour is lighter than chain mail |
| 36-70 lb | 90 ft/round | Normal bonuses apply |
| 71-105 lb | 60 ft/round | Normal bonuses do not apply, but penalties do |
| 106-150 lb | 30 ft/round | Normal bonuses do not apply, penalties do, and an extra -1 penalty applies |
| More than adjusted limit | No movement | Cannot move |

## Armour Bulk

Armour sets a maximum movement rate independent of total weight. A lightly packed character in plate is still limited by the armour's bulk.

Magic armour is unencumbering and allows a maximum movement rate 30 ft faster than normal, up to 120 ft.

## Containers

A character needs a container to carry liquids, coins, and loose gear.

| Container | Capacity |
| --- | ---: |
| Small pouch or purse | 1/4 cu ft or 2.5 lb |
| Large pouch | 1/2 cu ft or 5 lb |
| Small sack | 1 cu ft or 10 lb |
| Backpack | 3 cu ft or 30 lb |
| Large sack | 4 cu ft or 40 lb |
| Waterskin | 3 pints |

## Example At The Table

A fighter carries 42 lb of equipment. With normal Strength, the fighter moves at 90 ft. If that fighter later carries 500 coins, add 50 lb. The new total is 92 lb, dropping movement to 60 ft and removing normal surprise bonuses.
