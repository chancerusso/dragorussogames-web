# Light, Vision & Infravision

Light is survival equipment. In OSRIC dungeons, the party sees only what its light, magic, or special vision allows.

Light also gives the party away.

## Light Sources

| Source | Illumination | Duration |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Bullseye lantern | 80 ft beam, 10 ft wide | 1 pint oil per 4 hours |
| Hooded lantern | 30 ft radius | 1 pint oil per 4 hours |
| Torch | 40 ft radius | 6 turns |
| Standard candle | 20 ft radius | 30 minutes |
| Magical dagger | 10 ft | No fuel limit |
| Magical longsword | 20 ft | No fuel limit |

Other light sources are adjudicated by the referee using these values as guidelines.

## Being Seen

Light sources can be seen from much farther away than they illuminate. Intelligent creatures may notice approaching light and prepare.

If dungeon creatures can see the party's light, the referee should adjust surprise chances.

## Infravision

Infravision is the ability to see in the dark. It is common among subterranean creatures.

Unless stated otherwise, infravision has a 60 ft range. It cannot be used inside the area of a light source. It does not show colours and is not very useful for searching or minute examination.

Even creatures with infravision may prefer torchlight for detailed work.

## Example At The Table

The party carries a hooded lantern. They can see in a 30 ft radius, but creatures down a long corridor may see the lantern glow before the party sees them. The referee adjusts surprise because the party announced itself with light.
