# Falling

Falling damage in OSRIC increases sharply with distance. Even a short pit can kill a wounded character.

## Falling Damage

| Distance Fallen | Damage |
| ---: | ---: |
| Less than 5 ft | No damage |
| Up to 10 ft | 1d6 |
| Up to 20 ft | 3d6 |
| Up to 30 ft | 6d6 |
| Up to 40 ft | 10d6 |
| Up to 50 ft | 15d6 |
| More than 50 ft | 20d6 |

## Saving Throw Option

OSRIC notes that a kindly referee may allow a saving throw against falling damage. If allowed and the save succeeds, damage is halved.

This is an optional mercy, not the baseline guarantee.

## Gear And Falling

Falling can also endanger gear. OSRIC includes item saving throws for objects damaged by impacts and other hazards. Magical items gain bonuses to item saving throws.

## Example At The Table

A thief fails to notice a 20 ft pit and falls in. The fall is up to 20 ft, so the thief takes 3d6 damage. If the referee allows the optional falling save and the thief succeeds, that damage is halved.
