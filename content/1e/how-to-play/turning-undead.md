# Turning Undead

Clerics and paladins can turn undead by religious power. Turning can drive undead away or destroy them outright at higher cleric levels.

Evil clerics may choose to control undead instead of turning them.

## Making The Attempt

The cleric or paladin must hold a holy symbol. In most cases this prevents attacking in the same round.

Roll 1d20 on the Turning Undead table. If the roll equals or exceeds the listed number, the attempt succeeds.

If a turning attempt succeeds, the cleric may try again next round. If it fails, no further turning attempt may be made during that encounter.

## Turning Results

Normally, 2d6 creatures are affected. Paladins and Type 13 creatures are exceptions, with only 1d2 affected. A result of D affects 1d6+6 creatures.

Turning lasts 3d4 rounds. Turned undead move away at their fastest movement rate. They attack only if something directly blocks escape.
