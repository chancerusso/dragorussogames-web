# Character Creation

## Overview
Build a First Edition character by reading each page, making manual choices, and writing the results on your character sheet.

This is a rules-first character creation flow for paper sheets and table play.

## Build Your Character
Use these pages in order. Each page teaches the rule, shows what to decide, and tells you what to record.

## What You Need
- A blank or printable character sheet
- Dice
- A pencil
- These character creation pages

## What You Decide
- Ability scores
- Race
- Class
- Alignment
- Starting wealth
- Equipment
- Hit points
- Languages

## What Comes Next
After Languages, bring the finished sheet to the table and let the referee confirm any campaign-specific details.
